Services

We build AI features and the software around them. Six kinds of work, described plainly, so you can see which one fits.

Custom AI and LLM applications

Software that puts a language model to work on your own documents, data and processes.

What we build

  • Document question-answering over your internal files, with source citations
  • Drafting and summarising tools for support, sales or operations teams
  • Classification and extraction that turn free text into structured records

Who it suits

Teams with a lot of text and a repeatable task, such as support, legal, operations or research.

AI agents and workflow automation

Systems that carry out multi-step work on their own, within limits you set, and hand off to a person when unsure.

What we build

  • Agents that triage inbound requests and route or answer them
  • Back-office automations that move data between your existing tools
  • Review queues where a person approves what the agent proposes

Who it suits

Businesses with manual, rule-heavy processes that cost staff hours every week.

Computer vision

Models that read images and video so a camera can count, inspect or recognise what matters to you.

What we build

  • Defect and quality inspection from line or handheld cameras
  • Document and ID reading, including forms in Indian languages
  • Counting and detection for retail, logistics or site monitoring

Who it suits

Manufacturers, retailers and operators who already have images or video and no good way to use them.

Data and machine-learning pipelines

The plumbing and models that turn scattered data into something you can trust and predict from.

What we build

  • Ingestion and cleaning pipelines with checks that flag bad data
  • Forecasting and scoring models, trained and evaluated on your history
  • Dashboards and APIs that deliver model output to the people who use it

Who it suits

Companies with data in spreadsheets, databases and exports that nobody has joined up yet.

Web and mobile software

Ordinary, well-built applications, with AI added where it helps and left out where it does not.

What we build

  • Customer portals and internal tools on the web
  • Android and iOS apps for field staff or customers
  • Integrations with payment, messaging and accounting systems

Who it suits

Founders and teams who need a working product, not only a model behind an API.

AI consulting and prototyping

A short, hands-on look at whether AI fits your problem, ending in a working prototype or a clear no.

What we build

  • A feasibility review of your idea, data and risks
  • A prototype you can test with real users in a few weeks
  • A written plan for build, hosting and ongoing cost

Who it suits

Teams weighing an AI idea who want evidence before committing a budget.

How we work with you

Pick the shape that matches how clear the problem is. We can move from one to another as the work changes.

Fixed-scope project
A defined outcome, a written scope and an agreed timeline. Best when you know what you want built.
Dedicated team
Engineers who work as an extension of your team over a longer stretch. Best when the roadmap keeps growing.
Advisory
Hands-on guidance on approach, vendors and architecture, without us taking on the build. Best when you have engineers and need direction.

Tell us what you are trying to fix.

A short description is enough to start. We will reply with questions or a clear view on whether we can help.
