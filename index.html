What we build

Six kinds of work, from a first prototype to a system your team runs every day.

All services in detail

  • Custom LLM apps

    Assistants, search and document tools built on large language models and your own data, with the guardrails to trust them.

  • AI agents and automation

    Software that reads, decides and acts across your tools, so repetitive back-office work stops landing on people.

  • Computer vision

    Systems that inspect images and video: counting, checking quality, reading documents, spotting what a person would miss.

  • Data and ML pipelines

    Clean, reliable data flows and models that retrain and monitor themselves, from raw files to a usable prediction.

  • Web and mobile software

    The apps, dashboards and APIs your AI features live in, built to be fast, accessible and easy to maintain.

  • AI consulting

    An honest view on where AI will help your business and where it will not, before you spend on building anything.

How we work

Four stages, in order. You see working software early and often.

  1. Discover

    We learn your process, your data and what success means, then agree on a scope that is small enough to finish.

  2. Prototype

    We build a working version quickly and put it in front of real users, so the risky questions get answered early.

  3. Ship

    We harden the prototype, test it, deploy it, and hand over code and documentation you own.

  4. Support

    We monitor, fix and improve after launch, and train your team to run it.
