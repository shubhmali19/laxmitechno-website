Laxmi Techno is a software company in India. We design and build AI products and the systems around them, and we are plain about what they can and cannot do.

Most of the AI work we are asked for starts as a demo that looked good once. Our job is the part after that: making it work on real data, for real users, every day.

We work with teams that want a product, an internal tool, or an automation built properly. We write the software, connect the models, test the failure cases, and hand over something your team can run.

How we work

Four commitments we hold ourselves to on every project.

  • Ship small, ship early

    We would rather put a narrow version in front of you in a couple of weeks than show a finished plan in three months. A small working piece teaches both sides more than any document does.

    Each step is something you can use, test with your own people, and say no to. If the direction is wrong, we find out cheaply.

  • Say what the model can and cannot do

    Models make mistakes, and some tasks are not a good fit at all. We tell you early which parts of your idea are solid, which are risky, and which we would not build.

    Where a model is involved, we measure it on examples from your own work and show you the results, including the misses.

    If a rule-based script solves the problem better than an AI model, we will say so.

  • Own the outcome

    Delivering code is not the finish line. The question is whether the thing works for the people who depend on it. We stay accountable for that, and we say so plainly when something we built is not working.

    You get the source, the documentation, and enough explanation that your team is not locked to us.

  • Keep client data private

    Your data is yours. We collect only what the work needs, keep access narrow, and tell you which third-party model providers or services touch it before anything is sent to them.

    If your data should not leave your own environment, we design for that from the start.
